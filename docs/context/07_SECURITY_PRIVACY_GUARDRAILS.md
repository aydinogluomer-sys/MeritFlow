# Security, Privacy and Ethical Guardrails

## Forbidden Features

- Screen recording
- Keystroke tracking
- Mouse tracking
- Camera monitoring
- Microphone monitoring
- GPS/location tracking
- Hidden employee surveillance
- Automatic punishment without review
- Public shaming leaderboard
- Hidden bonus formula
- Silent period-end policy change

## Privacy Principles

- Data minimization
- Purpose limitation
- Role-based visibility
- Employee right to explanation
- Auditability
- Export logging
- Limited retention
- Support access approval
- Tenant isolation

## Sensitive Actions Requiring Audit Log

- Role change
- Permission change
- Scoring policy change
- Bonus policy change
- Manual point adjustment
- Task approval/rejection
- Bonus calculation
- Bonus period lock/unlock
- Dispute decision
- Payment export
- Support access
- User deactivation

## Legal Warning

This system may affect compensation/bonus. It must be reviewed by legal, HR and finance experts before production usage. The software should provide transparent calculation, dispute mechanism and immutable records, but does not replace legal advice.
